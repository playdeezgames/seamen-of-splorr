**The Tale of the Second Mining Level**

When dawn did paint the loading screen with light,
I strode unto the copper veins once more,
With humble pick equipped and spirits bright,
Prepared to grind as grinders have before.
Each swing did ring like notifications clear,
Each sparkling node bestowed a modest gain;
The progress bar crept onward, year by year—
Or so it felt, through pixel, stone, and strain.

Then lo! A fanfare burst across the air;
The interface proclaimed in golden hue:
**"Mining Level Two!"** beyond compare,
As confetti briefly drifted into view.
The chat ignored my legendary feat,
No global message hailed my glorious name;
Yet in my heart there beat a triumph sweet,
Enough to kindle one more mining flame.

"O Copper," quoth I, "thou hast been my coach,
Thy humble rocks my personal gym became.
No speedrun led me to this bold approach;
I earned each point through honest click and game.
Though others chase rare loot with frantic pace,
Or flex their maxed-out builds in every town,
I stand with dust and triumph on my face,
A level richer, though of small renown."

So sing, ye playlists looping through the grind,
Bear witness, energy drinks by keyboard placed;
Record this milestone in the cloud's vast mind,
That no experience point be ever waste.
For every legend's saga must begin
With one small rock, one pick, one hopeful cue;
The epic starts not where the champions win—
It starts with someone reaching **Level Two**.
